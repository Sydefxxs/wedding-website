# Wedding site copy

## Voice
Warm, personal, lightly playful. Sounds like the couple talking to people they love. Not cheesy, not formal-stiff, no clichés ("happily ever after," "tie the knot") unless the couple asks.

## Rules by area
| Area | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Names + date + place. Nothing else competes. | **Ana & Luis** · June 12, 2027 · San Miguel de Allende |
| Intro | 2–3 sentences, why we're glad they're coming | |
| Events | Answer-first: time, place, attire, then flavor | **4:30 PM · Ceremony** · Jardín Rosa · Garden formal — heels sink, bring flats |
| Travel | Concrete numbers: airport codes, drive minutes, deadlines | Fly into BJX (1h 30m drive) |
| FAQ | Question in guest words; answer in first sentence | *Can I bring a date?* — Your invitation lists everyone invited… |
| RSVP labels | Plain verbs: "Find your invitation", "Send RSVP" | |
| Confirmation | Personal + next step | "We can't wait to celebrate with you! Add it to your calendar →" |
| Errors | Say what happened and what to do; never blame | "We couldn't find that name. Try how it's written on your invitation, or text us." |

## Must-answer FAQ list
Dress code · kids · plus-ones · parking · shuttle · weather · ceremony start (arrive early?) · unplugged ceremony · dietary needs · gifts · accessibility · RSVP deadline · who to contact

## Placeholders
Unknown facts stay in brackets: `[Venue name]`, `[Hotel code]`, `[Shuttle time]`. Never invent real-looking facts. Copy goes into Sanity fields/seed, not components.
