## What separates the best from the rest

1. **Simple to use** — RSVP, directions and registry are always one tap away. Many put the RSVP button at the top *and* bottom.
2. **Answers guest questions first** — timing, location, attire, lodging, kids, parking; the great ones add weather, local things to do, drive times.
3. **About the couple** — big photos, an "Our Story," a gallery. It should feel like *us*.

## Recurring patterns
- **Sections:** Schedule, Travel & Stay, FAQ, RSVP, Registry, Gallery / Our Story
- **Logistics:** airports, drive times, hotel blocks, map link per venue
- **Type:** serif for names/headings + clean sans or serif body; script only as an accent
- **Color:** cream/ivory, sage, olive, pastels — or bold black/navy monochrome
- **Personal touches:** custom monogram/logo, illustrated schedule, the dog in the FAQ, scrapbook-style gallery, parallax + custom illustration

## Where we go further than template sites
Most of these are Squarespace/Wix/Zola. Ours is custom, so we can do what templates can't:
- Cinematic scroll storytelling (pinned timeline, parallax photos)
- Page transitions instead of hard reloads
- Personal RSVP: guest looks up their name, sees only the events they're invited to
- Add-to-calendar per event, live countdown
- Click-to-edit everything via Sanity Visual Editing
- Fast on phones (most guests will open it from a text message)

## What we're NOT copying
Learn the patterns; never copy another couple's photos, illustrations, copy or whole look.
